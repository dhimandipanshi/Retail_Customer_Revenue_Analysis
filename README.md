# H&M Customer Behaviour Analysis — Retail Strategy Summary

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates **3,900+ transactions across 1,000 customers** to understand key drivers of customer spending, loyalty, and product demand. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, customer behaviour was segmented across demographics, purchase patterns, promotions, and shipping preferences.

**Key Finding:** Subscription status, purchase frequency, and targeted promotions are the strongest drivers of revenue. High-value customers (subscribers, frequent buyers, and express shipping users) contribute disproportionately to total revenue.

---

## Business Problem

Retail growth depends on understanding **who drives revenue and why**. However, without structured analysis, H&M lacks visibility into:

- Which customers generate the most revenue  
- Which products and behaviours drive repeat purchases  
- How promotions and subscriptions influence spending  

**Key Question:**  
Which customer segments and behaviours should H&M prioritize to increase revenue and customer loyalty?

---

## Methodology

- Cleaned and prepared dataset using **Python (Pandas, NumPy)**  
- Performed **EDA and feature engineering** (age groups, purchase frequency)  
- Conducted segmentation analysis using **SQL (PostgreSQL)**  
- Built **Power BI dashboard** to track customer behaviour and KPIs  
- Analysed revenue patterns across demographics, products, promotions, and shipping

## Results & Business Recommendations

### Key Findings

- **Subscribers** have higher average spend and purchase frequency  
- **Age group 36–45** shows the highest average transaction value  
- **Top 5 products** drive majority of purchase volume  
- **Express shipping users** are high-value customers  
- Discounts do not consistently drive high-value purchases  

### Business Recommendations

- Focus on **subscriber growth** as the highest ROI lever  
- Build loyalty strategies targeting **high-spend demographics (36–45)**  
- Use **top-performing products** as campaign anchors  
- Optimize discount strategy to protect margins  
- Target high-intent users (express shipping) for premium upsell  

---

## Recommended Actions

- Introduce strong **subscription incentives** (exclusive offers, early access)  
- Launch **loyalty programs** for high-value customer segments  
- Prioritize **top 5 products** in marketing and merchandising  
- Implement **targeted discounting** instead of broad promotions  
- Personalize campaigns for **high-frequency and premium users**
